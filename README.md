# Frida
Frida is an ambient AI assistant built specifically 
for the ADHD brain.

Not a timer. Not another checklist. Not an app that 
requires you to remember to use it.

Frida listens to your day and catches what your brain 
drops — commitments, appointments, names, times. She 
surfaces what matters when you need it, breaks task 
paralysis with one single next action, and shows up 
as a calm presence when your nervous system is 
overwhelmed.

She runs in four modes:
👂 Listen — catches commitments from your voice
📋 Regulate — prioritizes your day, breaks the freeze
🧘 Calm — de-escalates when it's too much
🚀 Launch — one thing, right now, no list

She also connects to Apple Watch and Samsung Galaxy 
Watch — monitoring your heart rate in real time. When 
your body starts dysregulating before your brain even 
knows it, Frida notices and shifts into calm mode 
automatically.

No App Store. No subscription. Installs on any phone 
like a native app. Bring your own AI key — Gemini's 
free tier means Frida costs you nothing to run.

This is a beta. It was built by someone with ADHD, 
for people with ADHD. It's real, it works, and your 
feedback is literally shaping what it becomes.

Founding beta members get lifetime access at the 
beta price — locked in before public launch.

🌸 Built for the brain that drops everything.
